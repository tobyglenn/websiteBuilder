BEYOND POWER

VOLTRA I · pair

I’d wait for VOLTRA 2

I would wait before buying VOLTRA I. With Nano now competing in this space, I expect a VOLTRA 2 could be coming soon. That is my expectation, not a confirmed announcement or release date.

Green for you if…

You want modular, battery-powered cables, flexible mounting and the ability to take the units off the rack.

Red for you if…

You want a complete guided gym with a built-in screen, bench and minimal setup decisions.

One unit shown · two units pricedEpisode discussion

Two units + mounts + rack · Selected bundle available
$0 required for core access

What works

200 lb per cable, flexible mounting and specialized resistance modes.

The compromise

Rack and fixed mounts included. Rebolt the plates to change height; choose the more expensive sliding setup for frequent adjustments. Bench and site-specific anchoring are extra.

Cost breakdown, related models & sources

Two VOLTRA I units: 2 × $2,199 = $4,398. Fixed Rack Mount pair with 1-inch bolts: $109. Two Basic Handles: 2 × $39 = $78. Titan T-2 71-inch rack: $399.97. Total $4,984.97 before tax, checkout shipping and site-specific rack anchoring/labor. Fixed-mount hardware is included; changing mount height requires rebolting. This is not the Performance/sliding-mount bundle.

Rack cost and scope: Titan T-2 71-inch rack: $399.97, the lowest-priced compatible new freestanding rack verified here. Rack footprint 57 × 50 in; 2 × 2 in uprights. Taller 83-inch version is $20 more. Secure the rack for the intended cable forces. Site-specific floor anchors, installation labor, taxes and checkout delivery charges are not included. No bench, barbell or plates included.

Mount compatibility: Published dimensions match: T-2 2-inch tubing, nominal 1-inch holes and a 6-inch bolt-center span fall within the fixed mount’s ≤4-inch rack depth and 5.88–7.07-inch adjustable bolt spacing. This is a dimensional compatibility check, not a Beyond Power endorsement of this exact rack.

Sliding-rack option: Performance pair with 3×3 sliding mounts $4,994 + Titan X-3 flat-foot rack $719.97 = $5,713.97 before tax, delivery and anchoring. Lowest verified wall-folding route: Performance 2×3 bundle + T-3 folding $399.97 = $5,393.97, but wall hardware / labor are additional.

AMP

Amp

I would not buy

A single cable with a 100 lb ceiling, plus $36 every month for the full experience. For me, that is a poor long-term value against stronger, subscription-free alternatives.

Episode discussion
$1,795Amp + installation + 5 accessories
Official US price · Current offer
$36/mo · selected full service

What works

Different form factor for people considering wall-mounted systems.

The compromise

100 lb single-cable ceiling. Used MP2 units sold Jan–Aug 2025 have a specific arm-lock recall; verify repair status.

Cost breakdown, related models & sources

$1,795 includes white-glove installation and five accessory types. Membership is now $36/month, with a 12-month minimum; older $23/$25 references are outdated. Manual lifting remains without membership.

Used-device recall: The Dec 18, 2025 notice applies to MP2 units sold Jan–Aug 2025. Affected unrepaired units require the free inspection / repair. Already repaired units need no further action under that notice.

My buying opinion: A single cable with a 100 lb ceiling, plus $36 every month for the full experience. For me, that is a poor long-term value against stronger, subscription-free alternatives.
