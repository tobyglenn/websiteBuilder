Inside the game

More than a collection. A team.

Monstrum World puts the ranch at the center of a creature-raising adventure. Hatch or capture a companion, train it, look after it, then take your team into the world to battle and discover what comes next.

The published game description lists 126 species, 2,236 collectible forms, and a 40-chapter story across eight acts. The interesting part is the relationship between those systems: care prepares your team for adventure, and adventure brings home the resources for its next stage.

01 / Explore

A ranch worth coming home to

Training, rest, feeding, and cooking all have a role in caring for your companions. Coach Juniper’s kitchen lets you turn ingredients collected in the world into treats, dishes, and meals. Choose food for the care or recovery your team needs.

02 / Explore

Battles with decisions to make

Bring your rancher and two companions into speed-based Active Time Battles. Choose attacks, skills, items, and traps, or use the available automatic tactics. Elemental matchups, your team’s condition, and the timing of your choices all matter.

03 / Explore

Build the next generation

Training and care influence evolution. Fusion lets you choose parents, preview an offspring, and select inherited moves. The October interface update gives parent selection and the offspring preview their own steps on smaller screens.

Before you jump in.

What makes it a monster-raising game?

Your companions need more than a place in a collection. Training, food, rest, evolution, and fusion connect the ranch routine to the team you take into battle.

Where is the new cooking system?

Visit Coach Juniper at the ranch. The kitchen shows recipes, ingredient requirements, shortages, and the effects of the food you can prepare.

Can I choose the music?

Yes. The v48 update adds ten original themes alongside Go! Go! Monstrum! Choose a song in Settings or use Automatic for a shuffled ranch playlist.
