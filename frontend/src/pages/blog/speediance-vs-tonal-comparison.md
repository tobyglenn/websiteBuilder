## Why I choose Speediance

I own the original Tonal and chose not to buy Tonal 2. I also train on Speediance machines. My preference is Speediance: the freestanding design, workout workflow and ownership costs fit how I train. That is an ownership opinion, not a claim that I tested Tonal 2.

The [1.2 million pounds ownership video](/video/Kjwo6nYNceg/) documents why I still preferred Speediance. That volume is the historical total discussed in the video, not a live counter or a controlled durability test.

## Software matters more than the AI label

I care about adjusting exercises, progressive overload, cable behavior and whether a workout can be completed consistently. A marketing label does not establish accurate form correction or automatically optimal training. My [V3.1 review](/video/ZbEstduW90I/) shows both improvements and problems in the version I used; it is not a claim about every later firmware release.
