## What I tested

This is about the software and firmware on my Gym Monster 2S in the [February 2026 V3.1 demonstration](/video/ZbEstduW90I/). My original Speediance downstairs behaved differently. It is not a test of every machine or a statement that today's firmware has the same behavior.

## The cable stop and the retraction issue

In the video I pull the cable to a chosen position and confirm Safety Start. It then stops retracting at that position. That is a minimum retraction point, not a maximum cable-extension limit.

I also demonstrate slack when returning the cable quickly, and explain why I dislike the changed retraction behavior. The video establishes what I observed on my machine. It does not establish the engineering cause or prove that Safety Start required that firmware change.

## Why I usually did not use it

I prefer a controlled return through the range appropriate to the movement. In some setups, the chosen stop position can interfere with the return I want. That is an exercise- and setup-dependent objection, not evidence that every use reduces range of motion by a fixed percentage.

My personal workflow is not a substitute for the current operating instructions. Use the manufacturer's guidance for your machine, and stop a set if cable behavior is unpredictable rather than relying on an old video as a safety guarantee.
