## The difference shown in the video

In my [September 2025 demonstration](/video/ZspIOVs0XDo/), I press the side button and show the adjustable section lowering slowly. I contrast that with an earlier experience in which a moving part came down and hit my head.

That more controlled movement is the useful observation. I describe it as feeling like it has a spring, but the clip does not establish the internal mechanism. I also explicitly say I do not know whether the change began with Gym Monster 2 or with Gym Monster 2S.

## Keep the observation in context

The “about 30 lbs” comment in the clip is my estimate, not a weighing test. The demonstration does not prove that this mechanism prevents every impact or makes it safe to release a part above yourself.

Follow the current adjustment instructions for your model and support moving parts as directed. Do not recreate the accident I describe to check whether your machine behaves the same way.
