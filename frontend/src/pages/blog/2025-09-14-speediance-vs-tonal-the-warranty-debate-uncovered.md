## What this experience does and does not tell you

My experience influences my buying preference. It does not prove that every owner receives free parts, that a company must repair an out-of-warranty machine, or that current support terms are the same everywhere.

Before buying, check the written terms for the exact model, seller and country. Ask what parts, labor, shipping and diagnosis cost outside coverage, whether replacement parts remain available, and which repairs the manufacturer permits an owner to perform. Get the answers directly rather than assuming my experience is a service guarantee.
