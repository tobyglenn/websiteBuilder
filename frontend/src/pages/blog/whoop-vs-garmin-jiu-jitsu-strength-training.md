## My current choice

The Garmin Forerunner 265S is a green recommendation from me. I still wear WHOOP, but I would not buy it today; it is yellow in my [current wearables comparison](/compare/#wearables).

My [January 2026 video and transcript](/video/apsAL3x_V3k/) explain the training workflow behind those opinions. My preferences can change without making that historical account a new test of today's hardware or software.

## Why the smaller Garmin works for me

I chose the Forerunner 265S because I wanted a lighter watch for running and overnight wear. In the video I explain that I wear it to sleep because I want the daily suggested workout features informed by overnight data.

My older Garmin watches still worked when I discussed them. That is my ownership experience, not a guarantee that every Garmin will last a specified number of years.

## Grappling changes the placement question

A watch and a screenless sensor solve different problems. I found a chest strap uncomfortable for jiu-jitsu and discussed alternatives to wearing a watch on the mat. Placement must follow your gym's or event's rules and avoid hard objects that could hurt a training partner. A sleeve does not automatically make a wrist device acceptable.

Heart-rate-based strain can add context, but it is not a direct measurement of grip fatigue, technique or how hard a particular round was. I do not have a controlled accuracy comparison here.
