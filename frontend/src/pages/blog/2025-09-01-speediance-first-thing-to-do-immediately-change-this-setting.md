## The problem I was answering

In my [September 2025 video and transcript](/video/udS5Iu6SXWM/), I respond to an owner whose resistance stopped when they reached the programmed rep target, even though they could perform more reps. My recommendation for that situation was to enable Unlimited Sets.

## The setting shown in the video

On that software version, I open Settings, then Training Preferences, then Unlimited Sets. The on-screen explanation says the counter can continue after the target and resistance releases once you stop. Check the current instructions and controls on your own machine because software can change.

My example is a target of 12 reps with capacity for 15. Continuing the same set is different from starting the next set just to add the remaining reps. It can give the software more information about the completed set, but it does not guarantee a particular strength increase.

## My warm-up example is not a universal prescription

I also demonstrate using a Stamina preset around 20RM with a target of 13 reps and a 30-second rest. That is my example of configuring a lighter set through a preset. A predicted repetition maximum is an estimate; subtracting rep counts does not measure your actual reps in reserve.

I discuss reducing the base load after adding eccentric resistance. A 1RM estimate is not a weight you should expect to lift for 12–13 reps. Choose a manageable load and follow the operating guidance for your model.
